Join WSC

Join a community built to help you explore finance, develop transferable skills, and build relationships that support an entire career—not only your first internship.

The Member Experience

Preparation That Grows With You

Each member selects a track based on their interests. Rather than preparing students for one job, every track builds analytical, financial, and professional capabilities that transfer across Wall Street careers.

01 · EXPLORE

Four Specialized Tracks

Explore investing, deals, research, and corporate finance while learning how roles and industries connect. View the career paths.

02 · BUILD

Transferable Skills

Develop financial analysis, valuation, markets, research, credit, modeling, presentation, communication, leadership, and commercial judgment.

03 · CONNECT & ADVANCE

Industry & MBA Access

Connect with professionals, Cornell alumni, MBA students, deferred admits, and peers while exploring graduate business education and long-term career transitions.

Explore → Build → Connect → Launch → Advance. WSC prepares members for careers that can evolve from banking to private credit, research to asset management, or industry experience to an MBA.
